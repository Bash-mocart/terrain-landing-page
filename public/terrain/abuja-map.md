abuja-map.svg: a real map of central Abuja (lon 7.356–7.644, lat 8.98–9.16) in survey blue.
- Terrain: 20 m contours from Mapbox Terrain-DEM v1 (z12), lightly smoothed, traced with
  d3-contour; heavier 100 m index lines; land above 520 m tinted; Aso Rock (931 m) marked.
- Roads and water: Mapbox Streets v8 (z13) road and water layers, simplified. Major roads
  (motorway to tertiary) draw in outward from the Central Business District; streets fade in
  ring by ring; lakes fade in.
All animation is CSS inside the SVG and is off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
Map data © Mapbox © OpenStreetMap. Generator: scratch scripts dem.mjs, roads.mjs, map.mjs
(not committed); regenerate if the area changes.
